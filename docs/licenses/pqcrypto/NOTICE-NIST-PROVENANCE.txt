OpenLit distribution annotation (not an upstream license or copyright notice)

The pqcrypto-internals 0.2.11 source package includes a modified NIST seed
expander in cfiles/nistseedexpander.c and include/nistseedexpander.h.
We acknowledge the National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST)
as the source of the original seed-expander software. The full permission
notice from NIST's December 2021 KAT source archive is included separately.

The dependency retains the original attribution to Lawrence E. Bassham and
states that Sebastian Verschoor modified the software for PQClean. PQClean's
2020-04-01 commit 33232a0343c75947e5479c622930461a800ec7d9 introduced the
common seed-expander files and records changes to maximum-length handling.
Its 2020-04-06 commit 9944e6a81d3edd2dbc191a818a827086b3385ba8 renamed the
AES key-expansion interface. Rustpq extracted the shared cryptography into
pqcrypto-internals in commit fb4ea774b1c5e1233a826779cad5304c06a23b93 on
2021-07-28. OpenLit has not modified these dependency source files.
